What does modernization without migration actually mean for enterprise architecture? Modernization without migration focuses on strengthening integration governance, API exposure, and runtime observability around existing systems rather than replacing them outright. By reinforcing the digital core, enterprises can introduce new capabilities without destabilizing revenue-critical transaction environments.
