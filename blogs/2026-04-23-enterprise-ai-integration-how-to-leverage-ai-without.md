What Is Enterprise AI Integration and How Can Organizations Use AI Without Replatforming? Enterprise AI integration is the practice of embedding AI models into existing systems through governed integration layers, enabling intelligent automation without replacing core infrastructure. Artificial intelligence has rapidly become a centerpiece of enterprise modernization strategies.
